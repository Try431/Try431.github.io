Ramblings of a Not-Quite Photographer

    Howdy!

    My name is Isaac - I've always had a penchant for taking pictures of the things and people around me, as well as for talking way too much, and hopefully this website will serve as a (constructive?) blending of the two!

    I hold a B.S. degree in Computer Science & Engineering from MIT, and currently work as a Staff Software Engineer for Binti! When I'm not sitting behind a desk at a computer, I thoroughly enjoy most activities which take me outside, especially hiking. I moved to Seattle early 2024, and love being closer to the beautiful nature that's up in the PNW.

    If you'd like to take a look at some of my pictures, the galleries are in the menu on the left (behind the menu button on a phone). They're ordered chronologically, so the most recent stuff is at the bottom.

    Whenever I go on a longer trip, I try to take a bunch of pictures and videos, and some of them end up on this site to share - not all of them are hiking/outdoorsy focused, but there's usually at least something that gets me outside when I'm traveling!

    A general goal of mine is to go on at least one longer hike every year - while there are plenty of outdoorsy opportunities in the U.S., I definitely want to keep exploring international long trails in the coming years :)
